Point colour: major hurricane red, hurricane orange, tropical storm blue, depression or remnant pale. The map’s advisory number is the one carried by the geometry, which can trail the roster’s by one advisory for a few minutes after an update. Times in point labels are NHC’s. Exchange prices are the Yes-side midpoint in cents, as quoted at the time shown, not fee adjusted; the small grey dots are the 163 reference locations the exchange’s wind contracts settle at.
