Hurricane tracking

ForecastEx lists contracts on how many named storms and how many hurricanes form in the Atlantic season, how many major hurricanes form in a month, whether specific category storms make landfall in the United States, and major hurricane landfall by region.

While a storm is active, ForecastEx also lists its flagship Live Hurricane wind gust contract at population centers in the storm’s path and a contract on which location records the highest wind gust of the storm.
