Hurricane tracking

National Hurricane Center forecast tracks, cones of uncertainty and seven-day formation odds, drawn from NHC’s own roster and NOAA’s public GIS service, refreshed every half hour.

ACTIVE STORMS

Point colour: major hurricane red, hurricane orange, tropical storm blue, depression or remnant pale. The map’s advisory number is the one carried by the geometry, which can trail the roster’s by one advisory for a few minutes after an update. Times in point labels are NHC’s.
