LIVE-STORM WIND CONTRACTS, DELIVERY BY DELIVERY

While a storm is running, each reference location the vendor has signalled on gets a chart of its probability ladder as it moved from one delivery to the next, with the exchange’s price for the matching contract marked on the same axis, since a probability in percent and a price in cents share the 0 to 100 scale. The horizontal axis counts vendor deliveries rather than clock time. Nobody knows in advance how many a storm will produce or when it will dissipate, so a new delivery adds a column and never moves what is already drawn. The rightmost column is reserved for settlement from the first delivery and stays empty until the vendor’s final file arrives. The strip above the charts is one tick per delivery; drag it, use the arrows or press ← and → to step the cursor back through the storm, and every chart moves together. Stepping back also moves the price square to the exchange’s price as it stood at that delivery, so an old ladder is never read against today’s price. At the newest delivery the square is the current price. A cycle the vendor did not deliver is marked between the two columns either side of it, with the usual break slashes, because on an axis that counts deliveries a missed cycle would otherwise close up and read as though no extra time passed.

OTHER TROPICAL CYCLONE CONTRACTS

A point is red for a major hurricane, orange for a hurricane, blue for a tropical storm and pale for a depression or remnant. The map’s advisory number is the one carried by the geometry, which can trail the roster’s by one advisory for a few minutes after an update. Times in point labels are NHC’s. Exchange prices are the Yes price in cents, midway between the Yes bid and one dollar less the No bid (there are no sellers on this exchange, only bids to buy Yes or No), as published at the time shown, not fee adjusted; the small grey dots are the 163 reference locations the exchange’s wind contracts settle at. Hover a region, storm point, dot, tile or table row for its values; a click pins the box, Escape or a click elsewhere releases it.
