About this site

What it shows

Live displays of US government weather data for the stations that daily temperature contracts settle on: observed temperature against the forecasts that were standing before the day began, a national map, a forecast scorecard, and hurricane tracking. The displays are meant for people deciding whether to trust a forecast, so they show what each source said and when, and nothing is smoothed into looking more certain than it is.

Methods

Every forecast cycle is archived as published, because no public endpoint returns an earlier forecast. “As issued” means the source’s last cycle before local midnight at the station. Day boundaries are the station’s local calendar day through its IANA time zone. The map shading is an inverse-distance interpolation of the listed stations’ NWS forecasts and is labelled derived; it is not an NWS product. Nothing here is an official National Weather Service product, and nothing implies endorsement by or affiliation with NOAA or the NWS.

Freshness

Observations refresh every 10 minutes and forecasts every 30. Each page shows the time of the data it is drawing. If the data feed falls behind, the page says so and keeps showing the last data it has; if the feed cannot be reached at all, the page shows the last data this browser saved, labelled as such.

Affiliation

This site shows no live market prices. Where market elements appear they are labelled placeholders that exist to show layout, not values.
