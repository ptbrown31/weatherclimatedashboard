Weather prediction market accuracy

ForecastEx runs daily prediction markets on the high and low temperature in cities across the US. This page scores the ForecastEx prediction market against twenty alternative forecast systems, among them the National Weather Service point forecast, the National Blend of Models, the Aviation Forecast, statistical guidance from four model families, and models and ensembles from the US, Europe, Germany, Canada, the UK, France, and Japan, including ECMWF’s AI forecasting system. Each is described at the foot of the page. Every comparison holds the city, the day, and the lead time fixed, and scores each system against the temperature the station recorded.

DETERMINISTIC SKILL

One value per system, scored against the recorded temperature by hours remaining before the day ends. For the ForecastEx prediction market that value is the median of its price ladder. Under the error curve, how early each system locks onto the temperature the day ends on and stays there.

PROBABILISTIC SKILL

The whole distribution, for every system that publishes one, the ForecastEx prediction market’s price ladder and four ensembles, scored on the same contracts by hours remaining before the day ends. CRPS scores the distribution in degrees, the Brier score contract by contract in cents, and the reliability diagrams show how the prices behind those scores paid.

CITY MAP

Compares ForecastEx prediction market error to each alternative forecast system’s error at each of the 24 cities, over the time window a reader would actually check, morning of, evening before, or newsletter hours, with the difference and its uncertainty interval.

SCORECARD GRID

Summarizes each system’s mean absolute error and mean error on the high and the low at three leads, with a CRPS for every system that publishes a distribution, scored against both the official settlement and the National Weather Service’s climate report.

FORECAST SYSTEMS

Every system on this page. A deterministic system issues one forecast per run. A probabilistic system publishes a spread of outcomes as well as a centre, which is what lets the probabilistic skill figures score it on the same contracts as the market. Grid spacing, time step and update frequency are the provider’s published values for the run behind each row. The record is how far back this page scores it.
