Tropical cyclone markets

Pick an ocean. Each map opens its own view, with the storms the National Hurricane Center has active, the contracts the exchange lists over that ocean, and their prices.

Contract Details

  • Season count contracts settle on how many named storms, hurricanes and major hurricanes the Atlantic basin records.
  • Landfall contracts settle on a major hurricane, Category 3 or above, making landfall in a named region or on land within 50 miles of its border.
  • During a live storm the exchange lists a wind gust contract at each reference location in the storm’s path, and one pool contract on which location records the storm’s highest gust.
  • Storm positions, tracks, cones and formation odds come from the National Hurricane Center. The Central Pacific outlook is issued by the Central Pacific Hurricane Center and is not in that feed.
