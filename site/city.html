ADDITIONAL VARIABLES

A temperature forecast is the end of a chain that runs through moisture, cloud and wind, and these panels show the chain directly. Each tool’s hourly temperature, dewpoint, sky cover and wind is drawn under the station’s own METAR readings, so whether the day is following the forecast’s physical script can be read before the temperature settles it. Heating running ahead under clearer skies than forecast argues the high is underestimated; persistent low cloud and an onshore breeze argue it will not be reached.
