EVERY SCORED DAY AT THIS STATION

Every tool is read at one moment: six in the evening, this station’s own time, the day before — so none of them has seen any of the day, and none has had longer to think about it than another. The hours under each name are how far that tool’s standing run was from midnight at that moment: hourly guidance is fresh, a four-times-daily model is not, and that difference belongs to the products rather than to the scoring.
