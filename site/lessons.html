Two courses on using these pages to read the markets ForecastEx lists on weather, climate and energy. The first covers what the contracts are, what settles them, and how to read a station’s day against the market pricing it. The second covers sizing a position across a ladder of strikes, the contract families beyond daily temperature, and one day worked from the evening before to settlement.

Each lesson names the page it teaches and links straight to it, and each keeps its own address, so a single lesson can be sent on. These are the written versions of the Traders’ Academy courses of the same names, and they carry no forecast, no fair value and no advice, the same as every other page here.
