A proposed settlement framework. No contract settles on it. Daily high, low, peak gust, mean wind and precipitation at sixty-seven settlement locations, each the 2.5 km grid cell that holds a main city’s City Hall, resolved on NOAA’s hourly RTMA analysis (within the hour, precipitation from radar) instead of a station’s reports. Values are rounded once, half up, away from zero, and a value equal to the strike resolves Yes. The exchange’s current daily contracts still resolve strictly above or below a strike.
