Forecast scorecard

Each source’s forecast for the day, as issued before the day began, against the METAR extreme that was observed. Mean absolute error, bias (positive runs warm) and the share of days within two degrees. Click a station for its day-by-day record. Hover any cell for the detail behind it (the low side, the issuing cycle, the lead); click a cell in the overall or day tables to pin the box.

BY STATION

NWS is the day/night forecast product; NBM is the National Blend of Models’ own daily max and min; GFS MOS is a single-model statistical product with no forecaster editing; LAMP is observation-updated hourly guidance with a 25-hour horizon. NBM is the guidance forecasters start from when editing the NWS grids, so those two are related, not independent. The record begins the day this site’s archive started; it grows by one day every day.
